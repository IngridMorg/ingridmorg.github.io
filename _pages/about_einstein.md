Hello.
I am currently a PhD student at the University of Leeds where I research Non-Photorealistic Rendering (NPR) and temporal coherence. I am a part of the [Visualization and Computer Graphics] [https://vcg.leeds.ac.uk/] research group. I am supervised by [Dr. Rafael Kuffner dos Anjos][https://rafaelkuffner.github.io/] and [Markus Billeter][https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/computing/staff/8810/dr-markus-billeter].

I completed my bachelors and masters at the University of Leeds, studying Computer Science with High-Performance Graphics and Games Engineering. After which, I was awarded the UKRI EPSRC PhD studentship to study NPR. I am passionate about creating perceptually-informed and artist accessible NPR pipelines, and making them fast.

During my undergraduate, masters and PhD I have been engaged with teaching, primarily as a teaching assistant though I also mark work and assist in bachelors project supervision.

I had a brief stint at the University of California, Santa Cruz Tech4Good lab working on Natural Language Processing, AI and qualitative survey answer processing. Although my main interests lie in computer graphics, I am rarely one to turn down opportunities to broaden my horizons.
